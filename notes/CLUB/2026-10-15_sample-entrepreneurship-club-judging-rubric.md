# Entrepreneurship Club: Judging Rubric

## Key Terms
- **Rubric** — scoring criteria applied to every pitch.
- **Tie-breaker** — rule for equal scores.

## Notes
| Cue | Notes |
|---|---|
| Criteria | Problem clarity 25%, market size 20%, solution 25%, team 15%, delivery 15%. |
| Time limits | Five minutes to pitch, three minutes of questions. |
| Tie-breaker | Highest score on problem clarity wins. |
| Open question | Whether to allow slides longer than six pages. Vote next meeting. |

## Summary
Rubric approved with the criteria above. Judges receive it by email one week before the event.
