# Adjusting Entries

## Key Terms
- **Accrual basis** — record revenue when earned and expenses when incurred, regardless of cash timing.
- **Adjusting entry** — a period-end entry that brings the accounts up to date under accrual accounting.
- **Contra account** — an account with the opposite normal balance of its related account, such as accumulated depreciation.

## Notes
| Cue | Notes |
|---|---|
| Four types | Prepaid expenses, unearned revenue, accrued expenses, accrued revenue. |
| Prepaid insurance example | Paid $1,200 for 12 months on Oct 1. At Oct 31 adjust: Dr Insurance Expense 100, Cr Prepaid Insurance 100. |
| Accrued wages example | Employees earned $800 not yet paid. Dr Wages Expense 800, Cr Wages Payable 800. |
| Why adjust? | Without adjustments, the income statement misstates expenses and the balance sheet misstates assets and liabilities. |

## Summary
Adjusting entries never touch cash. Each one affects exactly one income statement account and one balance sheet account. Quiz next week covers all four types.
