# Inventory Costing

## Key Terms
- **FIFO** — first-in, first-out: the oldest costs go to cost of goods sold first.
- **LIFO** — last-in, first-out: the newest costs go to cost of goods sold first.
- **Weighted average** — cost of goods available divided by units available.

## Notes
| Cue | Notes |
|---|---|
| Rising prices, FIFO | Lower cost of goods sold, higher net income, higher ending inventory. |
| Rising prices, LIFO | Higher cost of goods sold, lower net income, and lower taxes. |
| IFRS rule | LIFO is not permitted under IFRS; it is allowed under US GAAP. |
| Consistency principle | Once a method is chosen, use it period to period so statements are comparable. |

## Summary
Choice of method changes reported profit but not cash flow (except through taxes under LIFO). Practice problem: compute COGS under all three methods for the Oct purchase schedule.
