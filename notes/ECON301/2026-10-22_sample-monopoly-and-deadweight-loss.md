# Monopoly and Deadweight Loss

## Key Terms
- **Market power** — the ability to set price above marginal cost.
- **Deadweight loss** — the lost gains from trade when quantity is below the efficient level.
- **Price discrimination** — charging different customers different prices for the same good.

## Notes
| Cue | Notes |
|---|---|
| Monopolist's rule | Produce where MR = MC, then charge the price on the demand curve at that quantity. |
| Why is MR below demand? | To sell one more unit the monopolist must lower the price on all units, so MR < P. |
| Source of deadweight loss | The monopoly quantity is below where P = MC; trades that both sides would value are not made. |
| Perfect price discrimination | Captures all consumer surplus and eliminates deadweight loss, but transfers the surplus to the firm. |

## Summary
Monopoly produces less and charges more than competition. The efficiency loss is the triangle between demand and MC over the lost output. Antitrust and regulation are the policy responses.
