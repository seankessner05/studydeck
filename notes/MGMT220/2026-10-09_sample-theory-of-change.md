# Theory of Change

## Key Terms
- **Theory of change** — an explicit map from activities to outputs, outcomes, and long-term impact.
- **Outputs vs outcomes** — outputs are what you deliver (meals served); outcomes are the change in people's lives (food security).
- **Assumption** — a condition that must hold for one step in the chain to lead to the next.

## Notes
| Cue | Notes |
|---|---|
| Steps in the chain | Inputs, activities, outputs, outcomes, impact. |
| Why list assumptions? | Most programs fail at a weak link, not at the activity; naming assumptions tells you what to test. |
| Case: microloan program | Output: loans issued. Outcome: income growth. Assumption: borrowers have access to a market for their goods. |
| Common mistake | Reporting outputs as if they were impact. |

## Summary
A theory of change forces a team to say how and why change happens. Assignment 2: draft a one-page theory of change for a venture of your choice.
