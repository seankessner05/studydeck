# Measuring Impact

## Key Terms
- **SROI** — social return on investment: the monetized social value created per dollar invested.
- **Counterfactual** — what would have happened without the program.
- **Attribution** — how much of an outcome is due to your program rather than other factors.

## Notes
| Cue | Notes |
|---|---|
| SROI formula | Present value of social benefits divided by present value of investment. An SROI of 3:1 means $3 of value per $1. |
| Why the counterfactual matters | Without it you may count outcomes that would have happened anyway. |
| Randomized trials | Strongest evidence, but expensive and sometimes unethical or impractical. |
| Limits of SROI | Requires putting dollar values on outcomes, which involves subjective judgments. |

## Summary
Good impact measurement compares against a counterfactual and is honest about attribution. Final project requires an SROI estimate with stated assumptions.
