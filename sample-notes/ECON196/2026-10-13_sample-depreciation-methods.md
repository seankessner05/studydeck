# Depreciation Methods

## Key Terms
- **Depreciation** — allocating the cost of a long-lived asset over its useful life.
- **Salvage value** — the estimated value at the end of the useful life.
- **Book value** — cost minus accumulated depreciation.

## Notes
| Cue | Notes |
|---|---|
| Straight-line formula | (Cost − salvage value) / useful life. A $10,000 machine with $1,000 salvage and a 5-year life gives $1,800 per year. |
| Double-declining balance | Rate is 2 × the straight-line rate, applied to beginning book value, ignoring salvage until the last year. |
| Which is higher in year 1? | Double-declining balance gives more expense early and less later; total depreciation is the same. |
| Journal entry | Dr Depreciation Expense, Cr Accumulated Depreciation. |

## Summary
Depreciation is allocation, not valuation. Methods differ in timing but total the same over the asset life. Never depreciate below salvage value.
