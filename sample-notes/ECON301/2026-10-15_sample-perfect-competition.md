# Perfect Competition

## Key Terms
- **Price taker** — a firm too small to affect the market price.
- **Profit maximization rule** — produce where marginal revenue equals marginal cost (MR = MC).
- **Shutdown point** — price below minimum average variable cost; the firm should produce zero in the short run.

## Notes
| Cue | Notes |
|---|---|
| Demand curve facing the firm | Perfectly elastic (horizontal) at the market price, so P = MR. |
| Short-run supply curve | The part of the MC curve above minimum AVC. |
| Long-run equilibrium | Free entry and exit drive economic profit to zero, so P = minimum ATC. |
| Economic vs accounting profit | Economic profit subtracts opportunity costs, including the owner's time. Zero economic profit still means a normal return. |

## Summary
In perfect competition P = MR = MC at the optimum. Profits attract entry, which pushes price down until profit is zero. Compare this to monopoly next week.
