# Career Fair Prep Workshop

## Key Terms
- **Elevator pitch** — a 30 second introduction of who you are and what you want.
- **STAR method** — Situation, Task, Action, Result, for answering behavioral questions.

## Notes
| Cue | Notes |
|---|---|
| Before the fair | Research five employers and bring ten copies of your resume on one page. |
| Opening line | State your name, year, major, and what role you want, then ask a specific question. |
| Follow up | Email within 24 hours and reference something from the conversation. |
| Dress | Business casual or better. |

## Summary
Preparation matters more than polish. Fair is in the Houghton House ballroom; check the employer list on the career portal.
