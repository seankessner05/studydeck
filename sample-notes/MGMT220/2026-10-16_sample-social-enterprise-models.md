# Social Enterprise Models

## Key Terms
- **Hybrid organization** — pursues both social mission and revenue.
- **Mission drift** — drifting toward profitable customers at the expense of the intended beneficiaries.
- **Earned income** — revenue from selling goods or services rather than donations.

## Notes
| Cue | Notes |
|---|---|
| Three models discussed | Embedded (mission and revenue are the same activity), integrated (overlap), external (profits fund a separate mission). |
| Example: work-integration enterprise | Employs people facing barriers to work; the product sale and the social mission are one activity. |
| Risk of the external model | Easy to separate, but the business has no direct tie to impact. |
| Guest speaker point | Measure beneficiaries reached per dollar to detect mission drift early. |

## Summary
The model you choose shapes funding, governance, and risk of mission drift. Embedded models align incentives best but are hardest to scale.
